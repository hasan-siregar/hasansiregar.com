## Important Work

One framework to think about work allocation is to assign important one to more experienced, senior people. But, implicitly it says the other work allocations are not important.

I think a fairer way to think about it is based on risk. Risk is defined as the probability of the work failed to deliver based on technical competency required, ambiguity, experience and many more.

In the first place, nobody should work unimportant tasks/projects/initiatives. So, anything that's not important, can be deprioritized. So we care only important ones.
